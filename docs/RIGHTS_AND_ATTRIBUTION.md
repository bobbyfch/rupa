# Rights and attribution

Rupa is an independent visual library. An image, public API, metadata index, or download button does not grant permission to reuse or redistribute an image.

## Apple catalogs

Apple catalog artwork is shown as source-only promotional material with a link to its Apple item. Saved favorites keep a reference; they do not copy the source image into the repository. Apple preview video remains hosted by Apple and is not saved or cached by Rupa. Apple and the underlying rights holder set the terms for promotional art and preview use.

## Game art

Libretro, xlenore, and VitaHEX provide game index metadata and upstream image links. Images remain reference-only unless the linked record says otherwise. Wikimedia Commons results link to their file pages, where per-file license details can be checked. SteamGridDB and LaunchBox are outbound discovery links and are not mirrored into Rupa.

## Ghibli and Makoto Shinkai

The Ghibli collection links stills and film information to official Ghibli pages. The official still-image notice applies on its stated terms and is not a general license for redistribution.

The Shinkai collection links film images and exhibition photographs to Makoto Shinkai Works. Exhibition photos are served remotely from the official portal; showing them here does not grant a reuse or redistribution license.

The Makoto Shinkai collection uses remote image references from official CoMix Wave Films film pages and links every film back to its official page. The official rights notice restricts unauthorized reproduction, modification, and distribution. Rupa does not store those files in the repository; display, download, and reuse remain subject to the rights holder’s terms. A non-profit purpose by itself does not grant permission.

## Open-license mirror

Only files with per-file evidence of an open license or public-domain status are bundled. See [Attributions](ATTRIBUTIONS.md), [Coverage](COVERAGE.md), and data/provenance/rights-ledger.json. Wikimedia files require individual license checks. NASA media may have third-party, logo, or endorsement exceptions; read the item record before reuse.
